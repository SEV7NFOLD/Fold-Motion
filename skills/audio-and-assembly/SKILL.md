# Audio and Assembly

## Responsibility

Coordinate sound cues, music, voice-over timing, shot assembly, synchronization,
and final encoding when the project requires audio or multiple shots.

## Inputs

- Approved brief/storyboard, canonical timeline, shot outputs, audio assets,
  licensing/usage constraints, and export specification.

## Method

1. Use one structured timeline as the source of truth for shot order, start
   times, durations, transition durations, audio cues, dependencies, and
   export settings.
2. Confirm source audio, edit rights, sample rate, channel layout, and timing
   before processing. Record missing or unverified details.
3. Align cues and voice-over to the canonical timeline; check synchronization,
   levels, clipping, gaps, and transitions with tools actually available.
4. Assemble only accepted shot versions. Revisit adjacent shots and audio
   whenever timing or duration changes.
5. Encode to the specified output and inspect metadata and audio presence.
   Listen to the output when listening review is part of acceptance and
   equipment is available.

## Output

Provide the timeline/assembly plan, source references, settings, checks, and
known limits. Do not claim audio was heard, synchronized, licensed, or
validated unless that was actually established.
