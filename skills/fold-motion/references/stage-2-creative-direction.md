# Stage 2: Creative direction

Gemini proposes the concept inside the storyboard call (`title`,
`narrativeConcept`, `creativeDirection`). The director judges it at the
storyboard gate. Use this file to steer Gemini (through
`src/ai/prompts/storyboard-director.mjs`) or to review its concept.

## What good direction looks like here

- **One idea.** The single thing the viewer should remember, stated in a
  sentence. Every shot serves it.
- **Specific, not a mood.** "The name appears out of the dark like light on
  black glass" is direction; "premium and modern" is not.
- **Built from what renders.** The visual language of FOLD Motion is
  typography, solid shapes, the brand background, the accent glow and rings,
  and the built-in fade, rise and glitch. Strong direction uses those well
  (scale contrast, negative space, a disciplined accent) instead of wishing
  for footage.
- **Restrained accent.** The accent color guides the eye; one or two uses per
  shot.
- **Pacing with intent.** Let the opening breathe, keep the middle tight, hold
  the ending so the message lands.
- **Honest to the brand.** Use the client's colors and exact copy; don't
  borrow another brand's signature look.

## Reviewing a concept

Ask: Would two different briefs have produced this same concept? If yes, it's
generic. Does the direction promise anything outside
[engine.md](engine.md)? Does every choice serve the message? Record risks
and anything that needs the director's decision.
