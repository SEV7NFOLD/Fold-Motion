# Brief-to-Criteria

## Responsibility

Turn the supplied client brief and references into a structured, traceable
production specification before storyboarding or coding.

## Inputs

- Original brief and requested outcome.
- Platform and delivery constraints.
- Brand guidance, references, supplied assets, and known limitations.
- Clarifications already received.

## Method

1. Extract objective, audience, message, platform, duration, format, brand,
   references, assets, and acceptance criteria.
2. Separate immutable hard constraints (exact wording, product details,
   disclaimers, required scenes, dimensions, and limits) from creative
   preferences.
3. Record missing inputs, assumptions, risks, and asset/licensing questions.
4. Ask a focused clarification question when an ambiguity could materially
   change the work. Otherwise state the assumption and its consequence.
5. Produce an `acceptance.yaml`-compatible specification with mandatory and
   forbidden content, visual direction, text/logo requirements, export
   settings, shot-level checks, and overall creative criteria.
6. Make each criterion testable by a human or deterministic check where
   practical; do not claim a criterion is verified before evidence exists.

## Output

Return the structured requirements, hard constraints, preferences, assumptions,
open questions, risks, asset inventory, and acceptance criteria. Preserve exact
client wording and identify its source. Do not invent product facts, approvals,
assets, or technical requirements.
