# Delivery and Verification

## Responsibility

Confirm that the delivered export, editable project, assets, configuration,
and QA record describe the same accepted version.

## Inputs

- Accepted project and final export.
- Delivery requirements, asset/font references, QA findings, and version
  information.

## Method

1. Verify the final file exists, opens, and meets required duration, dimensions,
   frame rate, codec, and audio requirements using available deterministic
   tools.
2. Confirm the export corresponds to the approved editable project, timeline,
   assets, fonts, and configuration.
3. Check that required source files, asset references, project configuration,
   QA report, and known limitations are included.
4. Confirm unresolved critical defects are fixed or explicitly disclosed.
5. Keep human final-cut approval available and record whether it was actually
   obtained.

## Output

Provide a delivery checklist with verified/pending/failed status, evidence,
paths, and limitations. Do not describe an unperformed check as passed or
declare delivery complete while a required gate remains unresolved.
