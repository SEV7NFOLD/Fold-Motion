# Product Animation

## Responsibility

Animate product interfaces and feature demonstrations accurately, with a
clear hierarchy between the product and supporting motion.

## Inputs

- Approved product facts, UI screenshots/assets or interface specification.
- Storyboard, exact feature claims, target platform, and acceptance criteria.

## Method

1. Use the real product asset or a coded interface when accuracy is required;
   never use image generation to approximate exact logos, dashboards, or
   controls when source material is available.
2. Preserve UI labels, data, units, and feature behavior. Flag missing or
   contradictory product information.
3. Choose an interaction that demonstrates the feature and supports the
   narrative; keep attention on the key action or result.
4. Define product state as a deterministic function of local time. Avoid
   fabricated live data, unverified claims, and interactions dependent on
   playback history.
5. Review text at final delivery size and inspect the complete transition into
   and out of the product moment.

## Output

Provide a product-accurate shot/interaction plan or implementation and list
assumptions requiring confirmation. Do not claim a feature exists or behaves a
certain way unless the supplied source establishes it.
